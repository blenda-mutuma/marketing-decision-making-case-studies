# Marketing Decision Making: Case Analyses & Marketing Plan
 worked in a team through a series of real business decisions. For each case we defined the core problem, weighed the alternatives with the case data, and committed to a recommendation. The series ended with a full marketing plan for Gatorade.

## Capstone: Gatorade Marketing Plan
[`capstone/gatorade_marketing_plan.pdf`](capstone/gatorade_marketing_plan.pdf)

Gatorade leads the U.S. sports drink market, but consumers are moving toward lower sugar, natural ingredients, and fitness as a lifestyle. Our plan:
- **Repositioning:** a new positioning, *"Performance is Personal"*, aimed at health-focused millennials and Gen Z (ages 18–35) while keeping the core base of serious athletes.
- **Targets:** $8.5B in U.S. revenue for FY2026, +200 basis points of market share, and a 12% trial rate for new, healthier product lines.
